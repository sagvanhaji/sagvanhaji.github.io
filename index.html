About

This site presents my work in quantitative economics, data analysis, and empirical research. I am especially interested in understanding how statistical methods can be used to study labor market dynamics, individual well-being, and the economic patterns that shape daily life.

01

Research Orientation

My work is centered on empirical economics, with a strong interest in survey-based analysis, behavioral outcomes, and measurable drivers of job satisfaction and well-being.

02

Analytical Style

I value clear empirical design, interpretable models, robust reporting, and research that connects statistical evidence to meaningful economic interpretation.

03

Professional Goal

I aim to develop rigorous, policy-relevant, and data-driven research that combines quantitative depth with practical insight for academic and applied settings.
